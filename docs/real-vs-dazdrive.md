# Real trucks vs DazDrive (research)

Compact comparison of what production trucks do against what DazDrive does. [V] = opened the page/PDF, [S] = search snippet only, [U] = could not source (unverified). DazDrive values are the current defaults in `docs/*.md` / `cruise.cpp` unless noted.

## 1. Shift strategy (TrueShift)

**Real world.** Start gear picked from gross weight and grade (Volvo: 1st-6th, "Performance Shift" [V fact sheet]; Detroit/Eaton same, `docs/lowspeed.md`). Kickdown = a switch at the end of pedal travel that switches the whole strategy to max acceleration, downshifting "when suitable" (Volvo AMSO-AUT [V]); Eaton/Detroit performance mode returns to economy when the pedal is lifted [V lowspeed.md]. Drive modes: Volvo Economy / Standard / Performance / Off-road / Heavy-duty; Economy "some reduction in power", all fuel functions (I-Roll, PCC, Smart Cruise) are disabled in Performance; HD mode = hold the mode button ~3 s, mode survives engine-off [V]. Volvo "Hill Management": on a climb where the next gear has no power, the engine rpm is *limited* instead of upshifting, and the box waits for a gentler grade [V]. "Soft Cruise": cap speed uphill so the top is reached slower [V]. Engine-brake mode (Volvo Enhanced Shift Strategy): gear chosen for high revs; wheel brakes fill the gap *during* each brake-mode shift so the truck never accelerates mid-shift on a descent [V]. Old Scania Opticruise downshifted at ~2300 rpm with the exhaust brake button [S]. Creep: Volvo idle-governor creep with closed clutch 1-3 km/h; slip-clutch creep 5-10 km/h (patent); maneuver modes exit ~16 km/h; hill start aid 3 s on > 1-3 % (`docs/lowspeed.md` [V]). Eco-roll: DAF and Scania only go to neutral if the roll will last >= ~10 s, never so fast that the engine brake would engage; DAF ends eco-roll at 91 km/h where the brake cruise takes over [V eurotransport DAF, S oemoffhighway Scania]; Volvo I-Roll runs 1-3 km/h *below* the set speed, "up to 0.5 %" fuel [V fact sheet]. Scania: when braking to a stop at low speed the box goes to neutral and pre-selects the launch gear [S]. Clutch heat budgets with warnings (Detroit "clutch abuse", ZF "CL") [V lowspeed.md]. Auto-neutral 10 s after the parking brake (Detroit) [V].

**DazDrive now.** Start gear = game pick lowered on launch slip; kickdown pedal > 0.92 at >= 1.5/s, lands power-mid +100, needs 2 s since the last shift; modes E/D/P/M on the game key, Eco -75 / Power +150 rpm, reserve 1.2/1.3/1.5, predictive off in Power; learned hunting guard (strain integrator, lessons per grade class, fade 3 km); crest band 3-5 km/h "top reachable = no downshift"; engine brake keeps rpm >= lerp(1100, brake-range low, level), two gears at a time, retarder no downshift; creep 5 km/h, off at 17 km/h; Auto Hold with no timer, release by drive force; eco-roll where neutral loses <= 0.04 m/s², enter set -0.5..+2, leave at set -0.5; smooth stop; M mode = DSG-like.

**Gaps / ideas.** (a) Eco-roll is far tighter than any real system (-0.5 km/h vs I-Roll 1-3 under, DAF/Scania >= 10 s rolls): a "min roll time" estimate from the route grade instead of the 0.04 m/s² rule. (b) Hill Management: when the guard says "no pull", also cap rpm (no upshift *and* no rev-out) rather than only remembering the lesson. (c) Performance auto-return (Volvo TPM-AUTR / Eaton): Power drops back to Normal after N min or at a stop. (d) Clutch heat budget for crawl with a "CL" letter and forced exit. (e) Auto-neutral 10 s after the parking brake. (f) Wheel-brake fill during engine-brake downshifts on descents (brake request = lost engine-brake force for ~0.8 s).

## 2. Predictive cruise (PCC band, crests, dips)

**Real world.** Settings are driver menus, not fixed: Mercedes PPC upper tolerance +2..+15 km/h (was +4..+15), lower -1..-10 km/h, display showed "-6" at 80 km/h, overspeed on a descent allowed for max **40 s**, active from 15 km/h, up to 5 % saving [V motorequipmentnews, S roadstars, V eurotransport]. Scania CCAP "Standard": +4 % / -6 % of set speed (85 -> +3.4 / -5.1 km/h); Economy level 3 lets speed drop more and coasts longer, "not for busy traffic"; up to 2-3 % [V eurotransport, V trans.info]. MAN EfficientCruise: 4 levels, level 3 default = +/-7 km/h, level 4 +/-9, levels 1-2 for dense traffic [S man.eu]. DAF PCC: driver sets under/overrun, magazine used +/-5 km/h; look-ahead 1-2 km [S DAF]. Volvo PCC: accelerate before the climb, stay in the high gear, stop fueling before the descent, coast into the next climb, curves/roundabouts/speed limits from map [V volvotrucks.co.uk]. All systems let the truck *drop below* set before a crest ("rolls over the brow") and *rise above* set on a descent only when a climb follows. Driver complaints (trucknet, [S]): coasting before summits annoys following trucks; experienced drivers see no fuel gain over their own driving; car-world predictive systems get switched off for slowing 8-10 km/h on gentle curves.

**DazDrive now.** Band up = at most 6 km/h (`band_up_kmh`) and only as much as the next climb needs (TrueShift works out the extra speed that gets the truck over the top at full throttle; fallback: scaled by mass, 2.4 km/h solo, 6 at 40 t); band under = 5 x (0.6 + 0.4 k) (3-5 km/h), x0.6 in Power; momentum into a climb = set + band; dip (a descent with a valley ahead) = the game's smart overspeed raised to the band, capped at 40 s (`band_max_s`, as Mercedes PPC); no band with a lead vehicle within ~4 s; curve lateral acceleration 3.25 m/s² (`curve_lat`), coasting planned at 0.4 m/s², brakes at 0.9; junctions 25 km/h at 90 deg; ISA limit + 3, easing 2.5 km/h/s.

**Gaps / ideas.** (a) The band is sized by what the climb needs, so on flat or rolling terrain it is small, while OEM defaults are fixed tolerances (+4..+7 / -5..-7): offer MAN-style levels 1-4 (+/-3, 5, 7, 9) in the ini/setup page. (b) Economy level 3 behavior = larger under-band + longer eco-roll in Eco only. (c) Volvo "cruise acceleration limiter": a softer return to set speed in Eco (accel cap ~0.3 m/s²) instead of the full PI.

## 3. ACC / Stop&Go / Auto Hold

**Real world.** Gap settings: Detroit Assurance 5.0 default **3.6 s**, driver range 2.4-3.6 s [S Freightliner brochure]; Bendix Wingman 2.8-3.5 s, 4 display steps, resets to step 3 at ignition [S fleetequipmentmag]; MAN 4 levels, Mercedes 5 steps (no seconds published); Scania ACC engages above 20 km/h [S]. Stop&Go: MAN and Detroit drive off by themselves only if the stop was **< 2 s**; longer = service brake applied, driver taps throttle or resume [V man.eu, S brochure]. Volvo Downhill Cruise Control: max speed on a descent, auxiliary brakes first, wheel brakes "only when required", brake pedal does not cancel [V fact sheet]; "Smart Cruise Control" keeps the auxiliary brakes off on descents where I-Roll can be used [V]. Volvo cruise engages from 5 km/h (CRUIS-E) [V]. ACC deceleration limits: ISO 15622 is paywalled; the often-quoted 3.5 m/s² average limit is [U]. Volvo Launch Control: EBS brakes engage automatically on a direction change and Auto Hold is on while maneuvering; "Change Direction" up to 30 km/h: accelerator brakes the truck and reverses it [V].

**DazDrive now.** Levels 1.0 / 1.25 / 1.5 s + 6 m (short gaps, the game is scaled; presets Relaxed/Spacious longer); own ACC gain 0.3, target falls 1.2 m/s², rises 0.8; own brake request <= 0.35 (~3.7 m/s² peak), plan decel 1.2 / heavy; Stop&Go always drives off (`stop_go_auto_s` 0); Auto Hold no timer; cruise from 5 km/h; descent mode via the game's smart overspeed.

**Gaps / ideas.** (a) Our default gaps are 2-3x shorter than any OEM; add a "fleet" preset (2.4 / 3.0 / 3.6 s) and make the 2020-era default >= 2.0 s. (b) `stop_go_auto_s` 2 would be the real behavior (the default 0 always drives off by itself). (c) Downhill: auxiliary brakes first, service brake only above set + offset (we already lean on the game; make the offset a driver setting like PPC 2-15 km/h).

## 4. AEB

**Real world.** UN R131 (mandatory N3): active from 15 km/h; warning at least **1.4 s** before the emergency phase (one mode) and 0.8 s (two modes: haptic/acoustic + optical); warning phase may reduce speed at most 15 km/h or 30 %; the emergency phase may not start before **TTC 3.0 s**; emergency demand >= **4 m/s²**; stationary target: >= 20 km/h speed reduction from 80 km/h, moving target (12 km/h): no impact; driver can interrupt with a positive action such as kick-down or the indicator [V EUR-Lex R131, V legislation.gov.uk 2015/562]. The 2023 02-series adds pedestrians (avoid up to 20 km/h) and "much stronger" braking for stationary objects [S CLEPA]. Mercedes ABA 5: warning -> partial ~50 % (~3 m/s²) with hazards -> full braking to standstill; pedestrians: partial braking at once [V/S mercedes-benz-trucks, carlist]. Scania: tone + red lamp -> gentle -> full; kickdown suppresses it for **3 s** [S Scania AU]. False positives are the real-world problem: NHTSA investigates 250k Cascadias for hard braking at bridges, overhead signs, guardrails in curves [S truckingdive].

**DazDrive now.** Warning TTC < 3.0 s or a_need > 1.5 -> partial TTC < 2.0 (~3 m/s²) -> full TTC < 1.2 (1.4 x a_need, 6.5 m/s² below TTC 0.8); min 15 km/h; only gas cancels (3 s); an indicator alone and a young target only prevent a start; indicator plus steering to that side (evasive lane change) stands a warning or partial stage down for 3 s; crossing traffic ends it; tailgating not nagged; heavy = earlier.

**Gaps / ideas.** Matches R131 well (15 km/h, TTC 3.0, partial ~3 m/s², kick-down 3 s). Differences: (a) R131 lets a positive driver action such as the indicator interrupt the system; here the indicator alone only blocks a start, and it interrupts only together with steering. (b) Our full stage (6.5) is above R131's 4 m/s² floor; fine for a game but make `aeb_max_decel` era-dependent (1995: 0 = off, 2010: 4, 2020+: 6.5). (c) Two warning modes: add the dash letter flash to the tone (R131 "two modes"). (d) False-positive guard like the real ones lack: require the target to be in our lane for >= 0.3 s is there; add "no AEB on parked cars while our path curves away" (curvature from the route).

## 5. Lane keeping

**Real world.** R130 LDWS: active above **60 km/h**, warning when a front tire crosses the marking, suppressed by indicator/driver intent [V EUR-Lex R130, S]. MAN Lane Return Assist: from 60 km/h, corrects only with the indicator off, torque always overridable [S man.eu]; Volvo Dynamic Steering LKA from 55 km/h, vibration instead of a tone [S volvotrucks]; Mercedes Active Drive Assist: lane centering at all speeds with ACC Stop&Go (jam assist), hands-off warning after ~15 s (reviewer), after **60 s** controlled stop in lane with hazards, cancel by kick-down [S roadstars]. R79 ACSF B1: hands-on systems, override force < **50 N**, warning cascade starting at the latest 15 s after hands off [S lawinsider, lievers]. Euro NCAP Truck Safe (2024) tests lane support that actively steers, AEB up to 90 km/h, ISA [S euroncap].

**DazDrive now.** Own law mode 2 (offset/heading/bend feed-forward, learned per-wheel profile, anti-sway, safety rule), steers from 20 km/h (game: 55) and can be switched on standing still; an indicator pauses it and it comes back after the lane change; no hands-on detection (strength by offset, not by hand).

**Gaps / ideas.** (a) Indicator pause + auto-resume is the R130/MAN behavior and is in. (b) Jam assist (lane + ACC Stop&Go at all speeds) = Mercedes ADA / MAN CruiseAssist: a realistic next step. (c) Optional hands-off cascade for "others" (15 s tone, 60 s stop + hazards), default off. (d) Era presets could differ in minimum speed: 2010 = LDW only (tone, 60 km/h), 2020 = LKA from 55-60 km/h, 2024 = centering at all speeds.

## 6. Real functions we lack and could build

Feasible in the game: hill-hold timer variant (3 s, 1995-2010 eras) next to Auto Hold; Volvo Change Direction (accelerator reverses below 30 km/h in crawl); clutch heat budget; auto-neutral on parking brake; performance auto-return; wheel-brake fill during brake-mode shifts; MAN tolerance levels; Scania auto-neutral + pre-selected launch gear when braking to a stop at low speed (we hold the gear with the clutch open). ISA: GSR 2019/2144 applies to N2/N3 too; the real systems warn or ease, override = press the pedal further (ours: gas wins, ok). Weather-adaptive gap: no OEM source found [U]. Platooning (ENSEMBLE, 7 OEMs): gap figures not sourced [U]; needs convoy data, skip.

## Ranked ideas (value for an ETS2 player x feasibility)

1. Realistic ACC gap presets 2.4 / 3.0 / 3.6 s ("fleet") and a >= 2.0 s default in the 2020+ era; the current 1.0/1.25/1.5 stays a preset. `[cruise]` ACC level seconds, `gen_ini.py` ERAS.
2. Stop&Go: drive off by itself only after a stop < 2 s, else pedal/resume. `[cruise] stop_go_auto_s` = 2 (the default is 0).
3. Predictive band levels like MAN/PPC (+/-3, 5, 7, 9) as a driver choice. `[cruise] band_up_kmh`, `[trueshift] crest_band_kmh`.
4. Eco-roll like I-Roll/DAF: enter when the predicted roll >= 10 s, run 1-3 km/h under set, exit before engine-brake speed. `[trueshift] eco_roll_*`.
5. AEB: indicator alone interrupts an active stage; era-dependent `aeb_max_decel`; dash flash as the second warning mode. `cruise.cpp` AEB.
6. Hill Management: rpm cap instead of a failed upshift on a climb (Volvo). `trueshift.cpp` hunting guard.
7. Wheel-brake fill during engine-brake downshifts on descents (Volvo Enhanced Shift Strategy). `trueshift.cpp` + `brake_request()`.
8. Performance auto-return to Normal after N min / at a stop (Volvo TPM-AUTR). `[trueshift]` new key.
9. Crawl clutch heat budget with "CL" letter + auto-neutral 10 s after the parking brake (Detroit/ZF). `creep.cpp`.
